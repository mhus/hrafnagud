Council approves long-delayed transit plan

The city council voted on Tuesday evening to approve the transit plan that had been under discussion for the better part of three years, ending a debate that had come to centre on how the northern line would be paid for.

Opponents argued that the cost estimates were optimistic, pointing to comparable projects in neighbouring regions that ended up considerably more expensive than their original budgets had suggested.

Supporters countered with ridership figures from similar cities. By their calculation the expansion pays for itself within twelve years, provided the federal contribution arrives as promised.

Construction is scheduled to begin next spring, once the planning approval procedure concludes. Several residents have filed objections to it.
